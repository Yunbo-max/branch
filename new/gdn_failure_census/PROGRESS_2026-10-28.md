## Finding: m-a-p checkpoints need a non-stock FLA (FLAME fork)

The three `m-a-p/*` checkpoints fail `validate_loading_info` with 24 unexpected keys
`model.layers.{0..23}.attn.D`, and their `config.json` carries extra keys
`qk_activation=silu`, `qk_norm=l2`, `use_beta=true`.

- The config `_name_or_path` is `/mnt/bn/ridger1/flame/configs/GatedDeltaNet/...`,
  i.e. these were produced by the MAP-Neo **FLAME** training framework.
- `qk_activation` / `qk_norm` / `use_beta` exist in FLA `delta_net` (not `gated_deltanet`),
  and the `attn.D` matrix is absent from official FLA `gated_deltanet` (checked 0.5.0 and `main`).
- Conclusion: the m-a-p "GatedDeltaNet" is a FLAME-specific variant, not loadable by stock FLA.
  The census README's "verified checkpoint identities" premise does not hold for FLA 0.5.0.

### Download notes (China network)

HF direct and `hf-mirror.com` are throttled (~30-150 KB/s single-connection). Fastest path
observed was ModelScope's CDN (`cdn-lfs-cn-1.modelscope.cn`), but the FLA-compatible GDN models
are NOT on ModelScope. For HF-mirror, the `hfd.sh` tool (aria2-based, resumable, multi-connection)
is the robust choice:

```bash

HF_ENDPOINT=https://hf-mirror.com ./hfd.sh <repo> --local-dir <dir> -x 8 -j 4
```
